# AI in our workplace

It has always been our choice as humans to steer the next generation of productivity and innovation. The very aspect of AI adoption and automation I am afraid of the most is when corporations outsource labor to AI, thereby abandoning human workers in a quest to maximize profits.

AI and advanced automation systems can accelerate innovation and growth by increasing productivity and efficiency. But in the wake of advanced AI, it has become increasingly important that we look for ways to share this labor. Human labor plus AI input can drive efficiency and productivity. It may not be able to match the profit margins created when corporations outsource to AI alone, but the importance of this process can define the future of society at a global scale.

## The deeper risk

It is a society where a few individuals and the corporations they control have absolute command of the economy through AI labor and technology, and where corporations care only about increasing profit margins.

Instead of “AI will take your job,” the more accurate warning is: “Corporations will outsource your job to AI to drive profit growth.” The alternative is to equip people with AI so that they become more efficient and productive.

The dystopia is not simply AI taking our jobs. It is corporations choosing to outsource human work to AI. The utopia is equipping human labor with AI to ease difficult work and drive productivity and efficiency.

## Equipping professionals

It is not only about automating medical procedures, but equipping doctors and nurses with AI to do their work more efficiently.

It is not about replacing software engineers with AI workflows, but equipping software engineers with powerful AI to build better software.

It is also not about replacing teachers with AI, but equipping them with AI to improve the way they teach.
