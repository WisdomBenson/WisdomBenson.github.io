# AI in our workplace

We have a choice about how AI enters the workplace. What worries me most is that corporations will outsource work to AI and leave workers behind in pursuit of higher profits.

AI and advanced automation systems can boost productivity and accelerate innovation. Workers equipped with AI can become more productive, although companies may see higher margins from outsourcing that work entirely to AI. How employers make that choice will shape who benefits from those gains.

If a handful of corporations control the technology that performs much of the work, economic power could concentrate in very few hands. When a company outsources a job to AI to increase its margins, its leaders are making a choice about whose work they value and who gets to share in the gains.

Employers can use the same tools to equip people for the work they already do. Doctors and nurses can use AI to ease parts of clinical work. Software engineers can use it to build better software, and teachers can use it to improve how they teach. I want people to use these tools and have a stake in the value they create.
