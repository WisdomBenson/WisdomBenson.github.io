# AI in our workplace

It has always been our choice as humans to steer the next generation of productivity and innovation. The very aspect of AI adoption and automation I am afraid of the most is when corporations outsource labor to AI, thereby abandoning human workers in a quest to maximize profits.

AI and Advanced Automation systems can accelerate innovation and growth by increasing productivity and efficiency but in the wake of advanced AI, it has become increasingly important that we look for ways to share this labor. I mean, human labor plus AI input can drive efficiency and productivity but may not be able to match profit margins when we outsource to AI alone, but the importance of this process can define the future of society at a global scale.

It’s a society where few individuals and the corporations they control have absolute control of the economy with AI labor and technology, where corporations only care about increasing profit margins. Instead of “AI will take your job”, it’s “Corporations will outsource your job to AI to drive profit growth” rather than equipping me with AI to make me more efficient and productive.

The dystopia isn’t AI taking our job but corporations willing to outsource my job to AI and the utopia is equipping human labor with AI to ease their job and drive productivity and efficiency.

It’s not just about automating medical procedures but equipping doctors and nurses with AI to do their work more efficiently. It’s not about replacing software engineers with AI workflow but rather equipping software engineers with powerful AI to build better software. It’s also not about replacing teachers with AI but equipping them with AI to better the way they teach.
