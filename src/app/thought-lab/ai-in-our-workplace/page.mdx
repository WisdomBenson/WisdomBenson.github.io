# AI in our workplace

I worry about workers who lose their jobs when corporations use AI to increase profits. Employers decide how to use these tools. Their choices affect who benefits from the work.

I see clear benefits in AI and advanced automation. These tools can help people produce more and reduce difficult work. I want employers to share those gains with workers. Human work supported by AI may produce lower profit margins than full automation. Even so, I believe this approach can support a fairer economy.

## The deeper risk

I fear an economy that a few people and corporations control through AI systems. This risk grows when employers treat profit as their only goal.

I question the warning “AI will take your job,” because it hides the decisions behind job losses. I use a more specific warning: “Corporations will outsource your job to AI to drive profit growth.” I support giving workers AI tools because this can preserve human participation and make difficult work easier.

## Equipping professionals

I want doctors and nurses to use AI to work more effectively and care for patients.

I want software engineers to use AI tools to build better software.

I also want teachers to use AI to improve their teaching.

I judge these uses by how they help people do valuable work and share its benefits.
